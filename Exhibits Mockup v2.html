EXHIBIT A · p. 3 of 24

Findings — Metro Division Compliance Audit

November 2016 · Office of the Auditor General · INTERNAL DRAFT

The metro audit findings of November 2016 identified seven distinct gaps in documentation across the procurement, occupational health, and contractor-onboarding processes maintained by the Metro Division.

Across the sample period (June 2015 – October 2016), the division processed 412 contractor engagements. Of these, 38% lacked a signed safety acknowledgment prior to site mobilisation, and 22% lacked the secondary signoff required under §4.2.1 of the prevailing OHS standards manual.

Beginning July 1, 2017, all metro contractors will be required to complete the new triple-signoff process described in Appendix C of this report. The transition period for legacy engagements ends December 31, 2017.

The audit team further notes a pattern of failure to maintain documentation supporting subsequent confined-space entries, which under the standards manual triggers an automatic suspension of the relevant work authorisation. Eleven such suspensions ought to have been issued during the audit window; none were.

Recommendations are summarised in Section 4 of this report, with implementation timelines contingent on procurement and contracting workflow changes detailed in the accompanying procurement RFQ exhibit.
